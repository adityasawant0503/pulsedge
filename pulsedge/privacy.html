Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

The short version. We collect your email when you join the waitlist, start a trial, pay or send feedback. Your watchlist and holdings stay in your own browser and are never sent to us. We don't sell data, run ads or use tracking cookies.

What we collect

  • Email address, when you join the waitlist, start a free trial, buy a plan or send feedback, plus the form you used and the time.
  • Messages you send through the feedback or contact forms.
  • Business and partner enquiries: your name, firm or community, designation, email, phone and what you tell us.
  • Partner applications: your name, email, WhatsApp number, city, college or work and how you'd find customers; and, once you're a partner, your PAN and the bank or UPI details we pay you to, which we keep for tax records.
  • Gift details: the name and WhatsApp number or email of the person a buyer is giving a plan to. Please give us these only with that person's agreement.
  • Telegram membership: when you join the private channel, Telegram shows us your Telegram name and username as a member.
  • Payment details, when you buy a plan: your name, email, WhatsApp number if you give it, the plan, any discount code you used and the UPI transaction ID you enter, plus the payer's name and UPI ID that your payment shows in our bank account. Your UPI PIN and bank login never reach us.

What stays on your device

Your watchlist and holdings, imported broker CSVs, theme, pre-market checklist ticks, trial status and access link are saved in your browser's local storage. They are not uploaded to us. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Why we use it

  • To run your trial or membership and send your access link.
  • To reply to you and send service messages, such as a trial ending or a renewal.
  • To improve the product from feedback.
  • To keep payment and tax records as the law requires.

We only email you about PulsEdge. You can ask us to stop at any time.

Services we use

  • Formspree: receives form submissions (waitlist, trial, payment confirmations, feedback, contact).
  • Your UPI app and our bank: carry your payment under their own terms.
  • WhatsApp: if you give us your number, we message you there about your access and renewals.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website and may log your IP address for security.
  • Google Fonts and cdnjs (Cloudflare): deliver fonts and scripts, and see your IP address when they do.
  • Telegram: runs the free PulsEdge channel and the private channel under its own privacy policy.
  • TradingView: the terminal and the live page load prices from TradingView's public data straight into your browser, so TradingView sees your IP address.

We don't sell or rent personal data, and we don't share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Waitlist and trial emails are kept until you ask us to delete them, or for up to 24 months after your last contact. Payment records are kept for as long as tax law requires (currently up to 8 years).

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, have it erased, withdraw your consent, and nominate someone to act for you. Send requests through the contact page. We aim to reply within 7 days.

Grievances

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, contact our grievance officer first; we acknowledge within 48 hours and try to resolve it within one month. You can also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

PulsEdge is for adults. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Security and changes

The site is served over HTTPS and we limit who can see personal data. If this policy changes, the date at the top will change, and we'll announce significant changes on the site.
