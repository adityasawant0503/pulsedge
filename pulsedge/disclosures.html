What PulsEdge is

PulsEdge is run by Aditya Sawant as a sole proprietor in Bengaluru. It reads public market news and exchange filings, matches each item to listed companies, and labels it Ignore, Watch or Act to show what may be worth reading now. It runs the website, the PULSE terminal, the free PulsEdge Telegram channel and a private channel for paying members.

Regulatory status

  • PulsEdge is not registered with SEBI as an investment adviser or research analyst, and it is not a stock broker, distributor or portfolio manager.
  • Labels are automated triage of public news. They don't take your finances, goals or risk into account, and they are not personal advice or recommendations to buy, sell or hold.
  • PulsEdge gives no price targets or stop-losses, manages no money, and never asks for access to your trading or bank account.
  • If PulsEdge ever offers research recommendations, it will register with SEBI first, and this page will show the registration number.

How labels are made, and their limits

  • AI models label each item. When no model is available, keyword rules do it, and the item says so. Act labels get a second AI check, and simple rules hold back items such as brokers' views, round-ups of several stocks and live blogs.
  • AI can be wrong, and sources can be wrong or late. Every item links to where it was published: read the source before you act.
  • Prices on PulsEdge come from free, unofficial public sources. NSE prices are about 15 minutes delayed and US futures about 10; the terminal marks which prices are live.

The track record

The track record checks how often a stock moved the way its label pointed, measured from the first NSE close after the news reached readers. It is a check on the labels, not a forecast or a promise. Past results don't predict future results, and nobody should expect to make money by following labels.

Conflicts of interest

  • The founder may hold, buy or sell stocks that appear on PulsEdge. Labels are made automatically from public news and are never changed to suit anyone's position.
  • PulsEdge takes no payment from listed companies, brokers or anyone else to publish, change, time or hold back news about a stock.
  • People who earn a commission for introducing customers to PulsEdge must say so whenever they promote it, as the Advertising Standards Council of India's guidelines require.

Advertising and prices
