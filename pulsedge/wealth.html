Built for a regulated firm

  • PulsEdge is a news and information service. It is not registered with SEBI as an investment adviser or research analyst, and it doesn't recommend buying, selling or holding any security.
  • Agreements are with the firm. We don't pay referral fees or commissions to employees or agents of SEBI-registered intermediaries, in line with SEBI's 2024 rules on associations with unregistered persons.
  • No client names, PANs or portfolios are needed. Watchlists stay on each adviser's device.
