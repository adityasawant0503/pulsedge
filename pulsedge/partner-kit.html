PulsEdge in one sentence

PulsEdge reads every NSE filing and market headline as it comes out, and tells you in plain English, within about a minute, which news touches the stocks you own.

The 30-second version

"You know how there's market news all day, and most of it doesn't matter to your shares? PulsEdge goes through all of it, NSE filings, results, big orders, global news, and sends you only what could affect the stocks you hold, with one line on why and a link to the source. It doesn't tell you what to buy or sell. It makes sure you don't miss news on what you already own. There's a free trial, so you can see it before paying."

Common questions

"Is this a tips service?"

No. PulsEdge never says buy or sell. It finds the news that matters for your stocks and explains it. The decision is always yours.

"I already get news free on Moneycontrol and Twitter."

You get hundreds of items a day there. PulsEdge reads all of them and shows only what touches your stocks, with the reason, often minutes before it trends.

"It's expensive."

With my code, Complete is ₹8,999 for a year, about ₹750 a month. There's a free trial first, and a full refund within 7 days of the first payment.

"I'm not good with apps."

The alerts come on Telegram, which works like WhatsApp. If anything is unclear, the founder answers on WhatsApp himself.

"Will it make me money?"

Don't promise that, ever. Say: "It won't tell you what to buy. It makes sure you don't miss news about what you already own."

"Can I pay monthly?"

Yes. Try a month, and if you move to a year within 30 days, the month you paid comes off the yearly price.
