How it works

  1. Apply with the form below. We reply on WhatsApp within two working days.
  2. A 15-minute call to walk you through the product, the rules and the seller's kit.
  3. Get your code and link, for example index.html?code=ASHA10. Anyone who opens the link gets 10% off on its own.
  4. Share it with people who invest: family, seniors at work, investing groups you're already part of.
  5. They pay on the PulsEdge checkout. The payment carries your code, so every sale is credited to you.
  6. Get paid by the 10th of each month, by UPI or bank transfer, with a statement of your sales.

The rules

Always

  • Say you earn a commission: "I earn a commission if you join with my code."
  • Show the real product: the free trial and the free Telegram channel.
  • Describe PulsEdge as news and information that helps people decide what to read.
  • Send people to the PulsEdge checkout to pay.

Never

  • Promise profits, returns or accuracy, or call alerts "tips" or "calls".
  • Tell anyone which stock to buy or sell, in PulsEdge's name or your own.
  • Take payment yourself, or offer extra discounts or cashback from your commission.
  • Message strangers in bulk, post fake screenshots or reviews, or say you work for PulsEdge.

Not open to employees or agents of SEBI-registered brokers, advisers, research analysts or distributors. SEBI's 2024 rules don't let them take commission from services like PulsEdge. Firms can work with us through PulsEdge for Business.

Programme terms

  • Who can join: anyone 18 or older living in India, with a PAN for payouts, who isn't covered by the line above. Joining is free.
  • What counts: a payment made on the PulsEdge checkout with your code, by someone who isn't already a paying member. Commission is 20% of what that customer pays in their first 12 months, and 10% of the first year of a business package you introduce.
  • Only your own sales. You earn nothing for recruiting other partners, and nobody earns from your sales but you.
  • When it's paid: once a payment is past the 7-day refund window. We pay by the 10th of each month for payments cleared in the month before. Refunded payments earn nothing.
  • Tax: commission is your income. Once your commission for the year crosses the limit in the Income Tax Act, we deduct TDS under Section 194H and give you the certificate.
  • Breaking the rules: we close the code. Sales made in breach of the rules don't earn commission; commission already earned fairly is still paid.
  • We can change the rates for future sales with 30 days' notice, or end the programme; commission already earned is always paid. Being a partner doesn't make you an employee or agent of PulsEdge. The PulsEdge terms also apply.
