      Live and late. Nifty, Bank Nifty and India VIX come from NSE's own feed, which runs one to three minutes behind its clock. GIFT Nifty, Asia, the rupee, the dollar index and gold are real time. US futures and Brent crude are ten minutes late. Each line says which: ● live, ○ late.

      The tiles refresh in your browser every 20 seconds (TradingView's public data) and use NSE's figures while they are under ten minutes old.

      Big money is NSE and NSDL end-of-day data; cash figures are provisional. It shows where money moved, not where it will go. A quick look, not for placing orders.
